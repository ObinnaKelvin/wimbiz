# WIMBIZ Women’s Health Screening Application

A digital health screening platform designed to support **WIMBIZ** in promoting women’s health awareness, early risk identification, and access to relevant health resources.

The application provides women with a convenient digital platform to complete health screening questionnaires, assess potential health risks, receive appropriate guidance, and identify when professional medical attention may be required.

## 🌸 Purpose

The platform is designed to extend the impact of WIMBIZ beyond the conference environment by providing attendees and other women with a practical digital tool for engaging with their health.

It aims to encourage:

* **Early health-risk identification**
* **Preventive healthcare awareness**
* **Routine health screening**
* **Personalized health education**
* **Timely referral to healthcare professionals**

## ✨ Key Features

* **Women’s Health Screening** – Digital questionnaires covering selected areas of women’s health.
* **Risk Assessment** – Analyzes responses to identify potential areas requiring further attention.
* **Personalized Guidance** – Provides educational information and recommended next steps based on screening responses.
* **Screening History** – Allows users to keep track of previous screening sessions.
* **Health Education** – Provides accessible information on preventive care and common women's health concerns.
* **Referral & Follow-Up** – Helps direct users toward appropriate healthcare services when further evaluation may be necessary.
* **Conference Integration** – Designed to support WIMBIZ events, campaigns, health initiatives, and participant engagement.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Because the application may handle sensitive health-related information, privacy and security are core considerations. The platform should implement appropriate safeguards for authentication, data protection, access control, secure storage, and responsible handling of health information.

## ⚕️ Important Disclaimer

This application is intended as a **screening and health-awareness tool**, not as a replacement for professional medical diagnosis or treatment. Screening results should not be interpreted as a definitive medical diagnosis. Users with concerning results should seek assessment from a qualified healthcare professional.

## 🎯 Vision

To leverage technology to make women's health screening **more accessible, convenient, and proactive**, while helping women become more informed and engaged in their healthcare decisions.

**WIMBIZ — Empowering Women Through Knowledge, Technology, and Better Health.**
